# Code of Conduct

Be kind, be patient and assume good faith. Mitra is built for people who find phones hard: that includes being gentle with newcomers to this project.

- No harassment, insults or discrimination.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Safety reports are welcome and handled privately (see SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may remove comments or contributors that break these rules. Report problems to the maintainer listed in SECURITY.md.
